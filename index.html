How to play

  • Roam the valley with WASD / arrows (or on-screen pad). The camera follows you.
  • Aim with the mouse/touch. Hold to draw — longer draw = farther & harder-hitting; a full draw adds bonus damage.
  • Release to loose the arrow. Intercept villains before they reach your gate at the west edge.
  • Villains close in from all sides. Drop them for gold; chain kills for a combo multiplier.
  • Clear a wave to open the Market: permanent upgrades (kept all run) + one-time special power-ups (keys 1–6). Grab Keen Eye for a dotted aim line.
  • Every foe at the gate — or rock that hits you — costs a heart. Waves are endless and scale up fast.
  • P pause · M mute
